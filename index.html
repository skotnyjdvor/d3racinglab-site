About Kart Republic

Passion into performance

Kart Republic builds racing kart chassis in Due Carrare, near Padova, and runs its own factory racing team. Since October 2025 KR is part of Korus, an Italian motorsport group built to cover the whole path from karting to single-seaters.

Racing Team · Dino Chiesa

The team manager behind world champions

The son of a karting pioneer, Dino Chiesa started as a mechanic and grew into one of the sport's most decorated team managers. He brought Lewis Hamilton and Nico Rosberg through karting, and today he leads the KR racing team.

TKART magazine placed him 9th among the most influential people in karting in 2015.
